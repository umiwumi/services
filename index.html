Suica/PASMO cards are NOT supported on local conventional trains (Joetsu Line). Purchase paper tickets at the ticket machine before passing through conventional gates.

Day-Use Onsen & Bathhouses

Historic hot spring bathhouses, ryokan day-use baths, and station sake hot springs.

⚠️ Tattoo Guidance: Public bathhouses allow tattoos. However, most traditional ryokans prohibit tattoos in communal baths. Request a private rental bath (貸切風呂 Kashikiri-buro) if available.

🌲 Recommended Stroll Spots (West Exit)

  • Free Footbaths (足湯): Soak your feet for free at the station West Exit plaza or outside the Ropeway station. Bring a towel!
  • Yukiguni-kan Museum (雪国館): 7-min walk. Explore traditional lifestyle exhibits and Yasunari Kawabata's literature history (¥500).
  • Suwa Shrine (諏訪神社): 15-min walk. Peaceful forest sanctuary home to massive sacred cedars over 400 years old.
🧳

Luggage Storage & International ATM

Coin lockers, manned baggage counter storage, delivery services, and overseas cash withdrawal.

🧳 Station Lockers Full? (Counter Storage) West Exit Info

If coin lockers are full, store bags at the Snow Country Tourism Center (West Exit, 9:00–17:00). Rates: Bags ¥500/day | Skis/Snowboards ¥1,000/day | Mobile Battery Rental ¥500/day. Same-day ryokan delivery cutoff is 12:00 PM.
