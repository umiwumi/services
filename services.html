🥢 Must-Try Regional Specialties
  • Bakudan Onigiri (Yukinto): Giant Koshihikari rice ball made with 1 full cup of freshly cooked rice and local fillings.
  • Hegi Soba: Silky buckwheat noodles bound with funori seaweed, served cold on cedar wood trays.
  • Ponshukan: Self-serve coin-operated sake vending machines featuring 100+ Niigata microbreweries.
💡 Tip: Outside winter ski peaks, most town restaurants take last orders around 19:30–20:00.

🌲 Recommended Stroll Spots (West Exit)
  • Free Footbaths (足湯): Soak your feet for free at the station West Exit plaza or outside the Ropeway station. Bring a towel!
  • Yukiguni-kan Museum (雪国館): 7-min walk. Explore traditional lifestyle exhibits and Yasunari Kawabata's literature history (¥500).
  • Suwa Shrine (諏訪神社): 15-min walk. Peaceful forest sanctuary home to massive sacred cedars over 400 years old.
