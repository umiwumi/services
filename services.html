🚲 Activities & Local Sightseeing

Explore the Kiyotsu Gorge Tunnel of Light and town attractions via official charter taxi or rental E-Bikes.

🚕 Kiyotsu Gorge Official Charter Taxi
Fixed-rate round-trip charter including wait time. Book at the Snow Country Tourism counter (West Exit) or call 025-785-5353.
🚌 Local Bus to Kiyotsu Gorge
Take the Morimachishako Line from East Exit (Stop 1) to "Kiyotsukyo Iriguchi".
⚠️ Critical: From the bus stop, it is a 30-minute (2km) uphill walk to the tunnel. Return buses are very limited.

🥢 Must-Try Regional Specialties
  • Bakudan Onigiri (Yukinto): Giant Koshihikari rice ball made with 1 full cup of freshly cooked rice and local fillings.
  • Hegi Soba: Silky buckwheat noodles bound with funori seaweed, served cold on cedar wood trays.
  • Ponshukan: Self-serve coin-operated sake vending machines featuring 100+ Niigata microbreweries.
💡 Tip: Outside winter ski peaks, most town restaurants take last orders around 19:30–20:00.

🚠
🌲 Recommended Stroll Spots (West Exit)
  • Free Footbaths (足湯): Soak your feet for free at the station West Exit plaza or outside the Ropeway station. Bring a towel!
  • Yukiguni-kan Museum (雪国館): 7-min walk. Explore traditional lifestyle exhibits and Yasunari Kawabata's literature history (¥500).
  • Suwa Shrine (諏訪神社): 15-min walk. Peaceful forest sanctuary home to massive sacred cedars over 400 years old.
🧳 Luggage Storage & International ATM
🧳 Station Lockers Full? (Counter Storage)
If all coin lockers in the station are full, manual baggage storage is available inside the Snow Country Tourism Center (West Exit) during operating hours (9:00–17:00), subject to remaining room space.
🧳 Coin Lockers & Luggage Forwarding
Lockers available inside Shinkansen concourse & CoCoLo (¥400–¥800). Hotel luggage forwarding is available at the Tourism Information Center (West Exit).
🏧 International Cash ATM (Seven Bank)
Many local bathhouses and soba spots only accept cash. 24/7 International ATM inside NewDays convenience store (Station 2F concourse).
