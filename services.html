🥢 Must-Try Regional Specialties
  • Bakudan Onigiri (雪ん洞): Giant Koshihikari rice ball made with 1 gou (cup) of freshly cooked rice and local fillings.
  • Hegi Soba (へぎそば): Silky buckwheat noodles bound with seaweed (*funori*), served cold with crispy wild vegetable tempura.
  • Ponshukan (ぽんしゅ館): Self-serve coin-operated sake vending machines featuring 100+ Niigata microbreweries.
💡 Tip: Outside winter ski peaks, most town restaurants take last orders around 19:30–20:00.
